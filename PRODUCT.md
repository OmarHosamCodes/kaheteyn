# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Charity administrators at a Palestinian charitable organization. They work in an office setting, often handling dozens of sponsorships, payments, and receipts daily. Arabic is their primary language. They need to track orphaned children, manage sponsors (kafeel), record monthly sponsorship payments (kafala), collect guardian acknowledgment receipts, and generate reports. The tool replaces manual spreadsheets and paper-based tracking. Speed and clarity matter more than visual delight; every wasted click is a real child's file not getting processed.

## Product Purpose

Kaheteyn (كهاتين) is an Arabic-language, RTL orphan sponsorship management system. It tracks children, sponsors, monthly payments (in USD), guardian receipts, and audit trails. It surfaces dashboards with alerts for overdue payments and missing receipts, and supports printable reports and CSV exports. Success means charity staff can process an entire month's sponsorship cycle faster and with fewer errors than their previous workflow.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, efficient. The interface should feel like a reliable tool that gets out of the way. Palestinian identity is present through color (green, red, black) and language (Arabic), not through decorative excess. No urgency, no drama, no fundraising theatrics. The work is serious and the interface respects that.

## Anti-references

- Not a SaaS dashboard with hero metrics and gradient accents
- Not a donation platform or crowdfunding site with emotional imagery
- Not a consumer app with playful illustrations or onboarding gamification
- Not a western-style admin panel that ignores RTL and Arabic typography

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** Every screen answers one question: what do I do next? Hierarchy, labels, and status indicators are immediately legible.
2. **Respect the work.** These are real children and real money. The interface is precise, never casual. Confirmation before destructive actions, clear status at every step, full audit trail.
3. **Speed is kindness.** Fewer clicks to complete common tasks. Batch operations where possible. Filters and search on every list. The tool should feel faster than the spreadsheet it replaces.
4. **Arabic-first, not Arabic-translated.** RTL is structural, not a CSS flip. Arabic typography, number formatting, and reading patterns are the foundation, not an afterthought.
5. **Restraint as reliability.** The Palestinian brand colors are present but not loud. The visual system is consistent and predictable so staff can build muscle memory quickly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA
- Full RTL support with logical properties throughout
- Arabic typography with Tajawal font (optimized for Arabic readability)
- Color contrast sufficient for all text and interactive elements
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- No reliance on color alone to convey meaning (status badges use text labels)
